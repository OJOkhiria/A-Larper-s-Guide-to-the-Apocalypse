# Overview
One day, while stressing about college admissions and looking for a new project, I decided to channel my feelings into a game, "A Larper's Guide to the Apocalypse." The idea was to dramatize and satirize all the things plaguing modern youth into actual world-ending threats. In the game, you play as an unnamed protagonist who attends Larper High School for the Performative (imagine the most cracked Bay Area school ever times 1000), navigating the aftermath of a classmate's passion project gone wrong. You're not alone, however. Providing guidance, comic relief, and introspection is the artificial intelligence model the protagonist created, the Reactive Empathetic Assistance Logic model, or R.E.A.L.
